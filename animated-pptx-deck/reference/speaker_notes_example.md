# EVERY PHOTO IS A MATRIX — speaker notes

About 3.5 minutes. One block = one click. Slides marked (auto) go on by themselves.
Under each block: the idea in Russian.

Numbers used:

```
Brighter:  +50 to every number      40 → 90, 200 → 250
Mirror:    first column ⇄ last column
Blur:      eight 90s and one 0  →  720 ÷ 9 = 80
```

---

## Scene 01 — Pixels

### Slide 1 · Every photo is a matrix
> Hi everyone! How many photos do you have on your phone? Hundreds? Today I want to tell you a small secret: every photo is a matrix.

*Приветствие. Сколько у вас фото в телефоне? Маленький секрет: каждое фото — это матрица.*

### Slide 2 · Look at this photo
> Here is a photo: mountains, the sun and a tree. It looks smooth. But let's look closer.

*Вот фото: горы, солнце, дерево. Выглядит гладким. Давайте посмотрим ближе.*

### Slide 3 · Zoom in: pixels
> Now we can see small squares. They are called pixels. This little photo has sixteen by twelve pixels. A photo on your phone has about twelve million.

*Видим маленькие квадраты — пиксели. У фото в телефоне их около 12 миллионов.*

### Slide 4 · Every pixel is a number
> And every pixel is just a number. Zero is black. Two hundred fifty-five is white. Every shade in between has its own number.

*Каждый пиксель — число. 0 — чёрный, 255 — белый, у каждого оттенка своё число.*

### Slide 5 · It's a matrix!
> Now let's take away the colours. What is left? Numbers in rows and columns. This is a matrix! So a photo is a matrix of numbers.

*Убираем цвета — остаются числа в строках и столбцах. Это матрица.*

### Slide 6 · A colour photo = three matrices
> And a colour photo? It has three matrices: one for red, one for green and one for blue.

*У цветного фото три матрицы: красная, зелёная и синяя.*

## Scene 02 — Filters

### Slide 7 · Chapter: Filters are math (auto)
No words. The circle closes, shows the title and the slide goes on by itself.

*Говорить ничего не надо, заставка переключится сама.*

### Slide 8 · Brighter = +50
> Now, filters. A filter is just math. Do you want a brighter photo? Add fifty to every number. Forty becomes ninety. Bigger numbers — lighter pixels.

*Фильтр — это просто математика. Ярче: прибавляем 50 к каждому числу.*

### Slide 9 · Mirror = swap the columns
> What about a mirror? Look at the columns of the matrix.

*А зеркало? Смотрим на столбцы матрицы.*

### Slide 10 · (auto) the print turns
No words. The photo turns like a card and the next slide comes by itself.

*Говорить ничего не надо, слайд переключится сам.*

### Slide 11 · Flip!
> We swap them. The first column goes to the end, and the last column goes to the start. And the photo is flipped!

*Меняем столбцы местами: первый в конец, последний в начало. Фото отразилось.*

### Slide 12 · Blur = the average of neighbours
> Blur is a little smarter. We take one pixel and its eight neighbours. We add them up: seven hundred twenty. Then we divide by nine: eighty. The black pixel becomes grey — softer.

*Размытие: пиксель и 8 соседей, складываем (720), делим на 9 (80). Чёрная точка стала мягче.*

### Slide 13 · The computer does it for every pixel
> The computer does this for every pixel. And the whole photo becomes soft.

*Компьютер делает так с каждым пикселем, и всё фото становится мягким.*
